# 4.5 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §4.5, pp. 106–107 ·
> [open PDF at p. 106](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=119) ·
> Up: [Chapter 4 — Anthropometry](./INDEX.md)

The chapter cites 19 works. They cover 1944–1998 cadaver anthropometry,
muscle architecture, and Winter's own balance studies. The ones worth
chasing for character physics are listed below.

## Key ideas — most useful references

| Reference | What it is | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Dempster, W. T. (1955) *Space Requirements of the Seated Operator*, WADC-TR-55-159 | Cadaver segment masses, COMs, inertias; the origin of Table 4.1 (via Miller & Nelson 1973 and Plagenhoef 1971) | the primary source when a Table 4.1 value looks off |
| Drillis, R. & R. Contini (1966) *Body Segment Parameters*, Rep. 1163-03 | Segment lengths as a fraction of height (Fig. 4.1); density vs ponderal index | proportion defaults for rigs |
| Contini, R. (1972) "Body Segment Parameters, Part II", *Artificial Limbs* 16:1–19 | Segment densities (Fig. 4.2) | per-segment density |
| Winter, Patla, Prince, Ishac & Gielo-Perczak (1998) "Stiffness Control of Balance in Quiet Standing", *J. Neurophysiol.* 80:1211–1221 | The 21-marker, 14-segment COM model; COP − COM = −K·COM̈ | balance controllers; COM estimation |
| Wickiewicz, Roy, Powell & Edgerton (1983) "Muscle Architecture of the Human Lower Limb", *Clin. Orthop.* 179:275–283 | Mass, fiber length, pennation and PCA of 27 leg muscles | strength ratios per joint |
| Hatze, H. (1975) *Eur. J. Appl. Physiol.* 34:217–266 | Simultaneous in-situ measurement of I, COM and damping of a segment | measuring joint damping and inertia |
| Elftman, H. (1966) *J. Bone Joint Surg.* 48-A:363–377 | Biarticular muscles as energy transferers | two-joint coupling in leg control |
| Maughan, Watson & Weir (1983) *J. Physiol.* 338:37–49 | Strength vs cross-sectional area; ~100 N/cm² MVC | absolute torque scale |
| Jian, Winter, Ishac & Gilchrist (1993) *Gait and Posture* 1:9–22 | COM and COP trajectories in gait initiation and termination | starting and stopping a walk |
| MacKinnon & Winter (1993) *J. Biomech.* 26:633–644 | Frontal-plane balance during walking | lateral sway in gait |

Others cited: Alexander & Vernon (1975, knee/ankle muscle dimensions);
Dempster, Gabel & Felts (1959, seated workspace anthropometry); Grieve,
Cavanagh & Pheasant (1978, gastrocnemius length); Haxton (1944, ankle
flexor force); Horak & Nashner (1986) and Horak, Nutt & Nashner (1992),
postural strategies; Smidt (1973, knee moment arms); Wilkie (1950,
force–velocity of human muscle); Winter (1984, gait variability and the
support moment).
