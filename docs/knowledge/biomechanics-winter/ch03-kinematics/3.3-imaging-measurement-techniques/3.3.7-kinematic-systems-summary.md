# 3.3.7 Summary of various kinematic systems

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §3.3.7, p. 64 ·
> [open PDF at p. 64](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=77) ·
> Up: [3.3 Imaging measurement techniques](./INDEX.md)

There is no best system, only one that fits a lab's requirements and budget.

## Key ideas

- **Clinical gait lab:** television — unencumbered subjects, qualitative
  replay, rapid turnaround, teaching.
- **Ergonomics and athletics:** instant or near-instant feedback to the
  subject, so an automated system.
- **Basic research:** turnaround matters little but many coordinates may be
  needed, so film or an optoelectric system.
- **Cost** of hardware and software is often the deciding factor.
