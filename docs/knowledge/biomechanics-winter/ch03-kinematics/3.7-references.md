# 3.7 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §3.7, pp. 80–81 ·
> [open PDF at p. 80](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=93) ·
> Up: [Chapter 3 — Kinematics](./INDEX.md)

About 20 references, mostly measurement-hardware history. The handful below
back the chapter's signal-processing claims, which are the part migera can
use.

## Key ideas

Most useful for animation/physics work:

- **Winter, Sidwall and Hobson (1974)**, "Measurement and Reduction of Noise
  in Kinematics of Locomotion", *J. Biomech.* 7:157–159 — the harmonic
  content of gait (99.7% below 6 Hz) and the forward-backward (zero-lag)
  filtering method.
- **Pezzack, Norman and Winter (1977)**, "An Assessment of Derivative
  Determining Techniques Used for Motion Analysis", *J. Biomech.*
  10:377–382 — the accelerometer validation of filtered finite differences
  vs polynomial fits vs raw differences.
- **Yu, Gabriel, Noble and An (1999)**, "Estimate of Optimum Cutoff Frequency
  for the Butterworth Low-Pass Digital Filter", *J. Appl. Biomech.*
  15:318–329 — Eq. 3.10, cutoff depending on $f_s$ and residual.
- **Giakas and Baltzopoulos (1997)**, "Optimal Digital Filtering Requires a
  Different Cutoff Frequency Strategy for Determination of the Higher
  Frequency Derivatives", *J. Biomech.* 30:851–855 — the optimum cutoff
  differs for displacement, velocity and acceleration.
- **Wells and Winter (1980)**, "Assessment of Signal and Noise in the
  Kinematics of Normal, Pathological and Sporting Gaits", Proc. 1st Conf.
  Cdn. Soc. Biomech. — residual analysis (conference paper; may be hard to
  find, and §3.4.4 reproduces its content).

Also cited: Gage et al. (2004) on the inverted-pendulum model of quiet
standing (source of the OPTOTRAK noise figure; its balance content belongs
to Chapter 11); Winter et al. (2003) on balance mechanisms in quiet
standing. The rest are hardware/history: Dinn et al. 1970 (CINTEL), Eberhart
and Inman 1951, Ferrigno and Pedotti 1985 (ELITE), Finley and Karpovich 1964
(goniometry), Furnée 1967, Jarett et al. 1976, Macleod et al. 1990, Moore et
al. 1991 (finger glove), Morris 1973 (accelerometry), Trenholm et al. 1972,
Winter et al. 1968 and 1972 (TV systems), Woltring 1987.
