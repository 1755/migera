# 8.8 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §8.8, pp. 222–224 ·
> [open PDF at p. 222](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=235) ·
> Up: [Chapter 8 — Synthesis of human movement, forward solutions](./INDEX.md)

The reference list has 21 entries in three groups: forward models of gait
and balance (the §8.1 review), classical and multibody dynamics texts, and
the software and graph-theory methods for generating equations of motion.
The ones below are most useful for physics-based character work.

## Most useful for animation and physics work

| Citation | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Gilchrist, L. A. & Winter, D. A. (1996). "A Two-Part, Viscoelastic Foot Model for Use in Gait Simulations." *J. Biomech.* 29:795–798 | Spring-damper array under a rigid foot; removes the heel-strike acceleration and GRF spikes of a pure spring contact. Read before building ragdoll foot contact. |
| Gilchrist, L. A. & Winter, D. A. (1997). "A Multisegment Computer Simulation of Normal Human Gait." *IEEE Trans. Rehab. Eng.* 5:290–299 | 3D 9-segment ADAMS walking model: spring joint limits, dampers everywhere, and the ~500 ms drift result |
| Wittenburg, J. (1977). *Dynamics of Systems of Rigid Bodies.* Teubner | Source of the segment KE formula (Eq. 8.23); a multibody-dynamics reference |
| Greenwood, D. T. (1977). *Classical Dynamics.* Prentice-Hall | Derivation of Lagrange's equations, constraints and multipliers |
| Wells, D. A. (1967). *Lagrangian Dynamics with a Treatment of Euler's Equations of Motion.* McGraw-Hill | Worked Lagrangian dynamics, the method this chapter follows |
| Hemami, H., Hines, M. J., Goddard, R. E. & Friedman, B. (1982a). "Biped Sway in the Frontal Plane with Locked Knees." *IEEE Trans. SMC* 12:577–582 | A small, successful balance model with hip/ankle actuators; relevant to lateral balance control |
| Onyshko, S. & Winter, D. A. (1980). "A Mathematical Model for the Dynamics of Human Locomotion." *J. Biomech.* 13:361–368 | 7-segment sagittal walker; the "two wrongs make a right" cautionary case |

## Other entries (by group)

- **Forward gait models:** Townsend & Seireg 1972 (*J. Biomech.* 5:71–83);
  Hemami 1980 (*IEEE SMC* 10:376–383); Hemami, Zhang & Hines 1982b
  (planar nine-link biped, *Math. Biosci.* 61:163–189); Pandy & Berme 1988
  (*J. Biomech.* 21:1053–1060); Chen, Hines & Hemami 1986 (right turn,
  *J. Biomech.* 19:195–206); Phillips, Roberts & Huang 1983 (swing motion,
  *J. Biomech.* 16:411–417).
- **Prescribed-trajectory / optimisation models:** Beckett & Chang 1968
  (minimum energy, *J. Biomech.* 1:147–159); Chao & Rim 1973 (optimised
  leg moments, *J. Biomech.* 6:497–510); Townsend 1981 (torso motions,
  *J. Biomech.* 14:727–738).
- **Equation-generation methods and software:** Andrews & Kesavan 1975
  (vector network, VECENT); Andrews 1977 (graph-theory restatement of
  dynamics); Singhal & Kesavan 1983 (vector-network mechanism analysis);
  Chace 1984 (ADAMS, NATO ASI F9); Paul 1978 (DYMAC).

## Where to read in the book
- pp. 222–223 (PDF 235–236): the full list. It ends at the top of p. 223. Chapter 9 starts on p. 224 (PDF 237).

## Relevance to migera

Only the two Gilchrist and Winter papers bear directly on migera's
ragdoll: the foot contact model and the drift and stability evidence. The
rest is history of the field, or dynamics textbooks that avian makes
unnecessary.
