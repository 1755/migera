# 8.2.1 Lagrange's equations of motion

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §8.2.1, p. 205 ·
> [open PDF at p. 205](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=218) ·
> Up: [8.2 Mathematical formulation](./INDEX.md)

This heading introduces the review that follows. The chapter uses
Lagrange's equations **of the second type**, written in generalized
coordinates. It does not derive them. For the derivation it points to
advanced classical-dynamics texts: Greenwood, *Classical Dynamics* (1977),
and Wells, *Lagrangian Dynamics* (1967).

## Key ideas

- "Second type" (second kind) means the form written in independent or
  generalized coordinates, with constraints handled either by eliminating
  coordinates or by Lagrange multipliers. Its counterpart, the first kind,
  works in Cartesian coordinates with multipliers for every constraint.
- The working review is split across the next four notes: coordinates and
  DOF, the Lagrangian, generalized forces, and the equations themselves.
