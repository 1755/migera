# 8.3.1 Segment energy

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §8.3.1, pp. 215–216 ·
> [open PDF at p. 215](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=228) ·
> Up: [8.3 System energy](./INDEX.md)

A segment's kinetic energy is translational plus rotational, plus a cross
term that appears only when the frame origin $pt(j)$ is not the COM
$pt(c)$. A segment is fully specified by its COM point index, its mass and
its inertia tensor.

## Equations and numbers

Kinetic energy (Eq. 8.23, Wittenburg 1977). Here $\mathbf v_j$ is the
velocity of the LRS origin, $\mathbf r_{jc}$ is the origin→COM vector, and
$\boldsymbol\omega$ is the angular velocity in LRS axes:

$$\mathrm{KE} = \tfrac12 M\,[\mathbf v_j]^t[\mathbf v_j] + M\,[\mathbf v_j]^t[\tilde\omega][\mathbf r_{jc}] + \tfrac12[\boldsymbol\omega]^t[\mathbf J][\boldsymbol\omega] \tag{8.23}$$

The first and third terms are the familiar $\tfrac12(mv^2 + I\omega^2)$.
The middle term vanishes when the origin is at the COM. Note that $[\mathbf J]$
must then be taken about the origin, not the COM (parallel-axis theorem).
For $\mathbf v_j$ and $\boldsymbol\omega$ to combine this way, both must be
expressed in the same (LRS) axes.

Inertia tensor about the LRS (Eq. 8.24):

$$[\mathbf J] = \begin{bmatrix} I_{xx} & -I_{xy} & -I_{xz}\\ -I_{yx} & I_{yy} & -I_{yz}\\ -I_{zx} & -I_{zy} & I_{zz}\end{bmatrix}$$

If the LRS lies along the **principal axes**, the products of inertia
vanish and $[\mathbf J]$ is diagonal.

Potential energy, with GRS $Z$ along gravity and zero level at the origin
(Eq. 8.25):

$$\mathrm{PE}_j = M g Z_c$$

where $Z_c$ is the $z$ component of $\mathbf R_c$.

Segment list (Eq. 8.26):

```
seg(i) = [c, M, Ixx, Iyy, Izz, Ixy, Ixz, Iyz]
```

- Principal LRS: drop the last three entries.
- 2D segment: the first three entries, $[c, M, I]$.
- Particle: two entries, $[c, M]$.
- Massless link: no seg list at all. Its LRS alone represents it.

## Relevance to migera

- The ragdoll's bodies are avian capsules. Their inertia comes from avian's
  collider shape at the density that `default_body_masses` sets
  (`ragdoll_plugin.rs`), so it is diagonal in the capsule frame and
  centered on the capsule midpoint. That is why the body frame is the
  midpoint frame
  ([ragdoll body and anchor frames](../../../character-animation/ragdoll-and-physics/ragdoll-body-and-anchor-frames.md)).
  A capsule's inertia is not a real limb's, whose COM is proximal of the
  midpoint (ch. 4). With the controller acceleration-shaped, this matters
  less, but it does change how bodies react to hits and to each other.
- Eq. 8.23 with the cross term is what an energy-conservation test needs
  if it measures velocity at a joint rather than at the COM.
