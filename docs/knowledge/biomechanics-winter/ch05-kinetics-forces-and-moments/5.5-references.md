# 5.5 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §5.5, pp. 137–138 ·
> [open PDF at p. 137](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=150) ·
> Up: [Chapter 5 — Kinetics: forces and moments of force](./INDEX.md)

The chapter cites 15 works. The ones worth fetching for migera's balance,
gait and ragdoll work:

## Key ideas

| Work | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Winter, Patla, Prince, Ishac, Gielo-Perczak (1998), "Stiffness Control of Balance in Quiet Standing", *J. Neurophysiol.* 80:1211–1221 | Full sagittal + frontal inverted-pendulum analysis behind Eqs 5.9–5.10; argues balance is largely ankle stiffness — directly relevant to a PD-based balance controller |
| Winter, Prince, Frank, Powell, Zabjek (1996), "A Unified Theory Regarding A/P and M/L Balance During Quiet Standing", *J. Neurophysiol.* 75:2334–2343 | Ankle control A/P vs hip load/unload control M/L |
| Gage, Winter, Frank, Adkin (2004), "Kinematic and Kinetic Validation of Inverted Pendulum Model in Quiet Standing", *Gait & Posture* 19:124–132 | Validation numbers (r = −0.954 A/P, −0.85 M/L) |
| Jian, Winter, Ishac, Gilchrist (1993), "Trajectory of the Body COG and COP during Initiation and Termination of Gait", *Gait and Posture* 1:9–22 | COM/COP trajectories when starting and stopping a walk — useful for start/stop transitions |
| Winter (1980), "Overall Principle of Lower Limb Support during Stance Phase of Gait", *J. Biomech.* 13:923–927 | Origin of the support moment |
| Winter (1984), "Kinematic and Kinetic Patterns in Human Gait: Variability and Compensating Effects", *Human Movement Sci.* 3:51–76 | Joint-level variability vs support-moment consistency |
| Winter (1983), "Energy Generation and Absorption at the Ankle and Knee during Fast, Natural and Slow Cadences", *Clin. Orthop. Rel. Res.* 197:147–154 | Ankle pushoff as the main energy generator; cadence dependence |
| Wells (1981), "The Projection of Ground Reaction Force as a Predictor of Internal Joint Moments", *Bull. Prosthet. Res.* 18:15–19 | Quantifies the FRFV error per joint |
| Scott and Winter (1990), "Internal Forces at Chronic Running Injury Sites", *Med. Sci. Sports Exerc.* 22:357–369 | Source of the 5.3.2 example |
| Elftman (1939), *Am. J. Physiol.* 125:339–356; Bresler and Frankel (1950), *Trans. ASME* 72:27–36 | Historical origins of force-plate gait inverse dynamics |

Others cited: Paul (1966) joint forces; Pedotti (1977) motor coordination in
locomotion; Hsiao et al. (2002) and Pitie et al. (1999) in-shoe pressure.

## Where to read in the book

- pp. 137–138 (PDF 150–151): full reference list.

## Relevance to migera

Winter et al. (1998) and Jian et al. (1993) are the two to read before
building a ragdoll balance controller or gait start/stop transitions; the rest
back specific claims in the chapter notes.
