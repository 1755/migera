# 5.4 Problems based on kinetic and kinematic data

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §5.4, pp. 136–137 ·
> [open PDF at p. 136](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=149) ·
> Up: [Chapter 5 — Kinetics: forces and moments of force](./INDEX.md)

Five problem sets run the chapter's inverse-dynamics recursion on the book's
own walking trial (Appendix A) and compare against the tabulated answers in
Tables A.5a (ankle, knee) and A.5b (hip). They are ready-made known-input /
known-output fixtures.

## Key ideas

The stride: heel contact right (HCR) at frame 28, stance frames 28–69, next
HCR at frame 97.

1. **Ground reaction interpretation** (Table A.5a): plot vertical GRF over
   stance against body weight and explain the two over-body-weight peaks and
   what the midstance dip says about COM acceleration; read the heel-contact
   sign of horizontal GRF; decide from the horizontal GRF whether the subject
   was speeding up, slowing down or steady.
2. **Ankle:** reaction forces (x, y) at frames 20 and 60 (compare with A.5a);
   ankle moment at frame 72 (early swing) and 60 (late stance), with
   functional discussion.
3. **Knee:** reactions at frames 20 and 60 from the ankle results; knee moment
   at frame 90 (late swing) and 35 (weight acceptance).
4. **Hip:** reactions at frames 20 and 60 (compare with A.5b); hip moment at
   frame 90 (late swing) and 30 (early weight acceptance).
5. **Synergy:** support moment $M_s = -M_a + M_k - M_h$ every second frame of
   stance from frame 28 (the minus signs make clockwise extensor moments at
   ankle and hip positive); then describe ankle, knee and hip moment polarity
   and magnitude over the full stride (frames 28–97).

Inputs come from the Appendix A kinematics (segment COM accelerations, angular
accelerations, marker coordinates), the anthropometric fractions of Table 4.1,
and the force-plate data in Table A.5a.

## Where to read in the book

- p. 136 (PDF 149): Problems 1–4a.
- p. 137 (PDF 150): Problems 4b–5d (support-moment definition).

## Relevance to migera

High as test data. Encoding Table A.5a/A.5b rows as fixtures lets an
inverse-dynamics helper (planar recursion of
[5.1](./5.1-link-segment-equations-free-body-diagram.md)) be tested against
independent published answers rather than against itself — the
[same-function-both-sides](../../engineering-practice/testing/same-function-both-sides-is-a-vacuous-test.md)
trap. Problem 1(c) (steady speed ⇔ horizontal GRF integrates to zero over a
step) is a direct check for procedural locomotion: an animated walk at
constant speed must have zero net horizontal impulse per stride.
