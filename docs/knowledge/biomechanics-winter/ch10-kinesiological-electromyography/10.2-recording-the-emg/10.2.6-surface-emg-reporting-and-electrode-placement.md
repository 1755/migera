# 10.2.6 Recommendations for surface electromyogram reporting and electrode placement procedures

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §10.2.6, pp. 268–269 ·
> [open PDF at p. 268](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=281) ·
> Up: [10.2 Recording of the electromyogram](./INDEX.md)

Surface-EMG results are only comparable across labs if the electrode setup is
reported; SENIAM is the reference standard.

## Key ideas

- First standard: ISEK Ad Hoc Committee report (Winter et al., 1980).
- **SENIAM** (Surface EMG for a Non-Invasive Assessment of Muscles), EU
  supported, reviewed by >100 labs 1996–1999 (Hermens et al., 2000);
  published as *SENIAM 8: European Recommendations for Surface
  Electromyography* (1999).
- SENIAM asks authors to report:
  1. electrode size, shape and material (Ag, AgCl, Ag/AgCl …);
  2. electrode type (monopolar, bipolar, 1-D or 2-D array);
  3. skin preparation and inter-electrode distance;
  4. position and orientation on the muscle (placements given for 27 muscles).
