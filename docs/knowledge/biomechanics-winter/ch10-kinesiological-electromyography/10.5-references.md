# 10.5 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §10.5, pp. 277–280 ·
> [open PDF at p. 277](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=290) ·
> Up: [Chapter 10 — Kinesiological electromyography](./INDEX.md)

About 50 references, mostly 1950s–1990s EMG physiology and instrumentation.
The list ends on printed p. 280 (PDF 293); chapter 11 starts on p. 281. The
handful below back the claims that matter for procedural animation.

## Most useful for animation/physics work

| Reference | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Milner-Brown, Stein & Yemm (1973), "Contractile properties of human motor units during voluntary isometric contractions", *J. Physiol.* 228:285–306 | Twitch as a critically damped 2nd-order response; source of twitch times behind the linear-envelope cutoff |
| Crosby (1978), "Use of surface EMG as a measure of dynamic force in human limb muscles", *Med. Biol. Eng. Comput.* 16:519–524 | 2nd-order twitch model; envelope-force correlation |
| Gottlieb & Agarwal (1971), "Dynamic relationship between isometric muscle tension and the EMG in man", *J. Appl. Physiol.* 30:345–351 | EMG→force as a 2nd-order low-pass: the activation-dynamics model |
| Calvert & Chapman (1977), "Relationship between surface EMG and force transients in muscle", *Proc. IEEE* 65:682–689 | Simulation vs experiment of EMG-driven force transients |
| Komi (1973), "Relationship between muscle tension, EMG, and velocity of contraction under concentric and eccentric work" (in Desmedt, ed., pp. 596–606) | EMG constant while force follows force-velocity |
| Komi, Kaneko & Aura (1987), *Int. J. Sports Med.* 8:22–29 | Less EMG for negative than positive work |
| Vredenbregt & Rau (1973), "Surface EMG in relation to force, muscle length and endurance" (in Desmedt, ed., pp. 607–622) | Nonlinear, angle-dependent EMG-force curves; fatigue recruitment |
| Krogh-Lund & Jørgensen (1991), *Eur. J. Physiol.* 63:60–69 | Fatigue numbers: −10% conduction velocity, −45% median frequency, +250% rms at 25% MVC |
| Fuglevand, Winter, Patla & Stashuk (1992), *Biol. Cybernetics* 67:143–153 | Electrode geometry, pick-up distance, m.u.a.p. spectrum vs depth (instrumentation) |
| Winter, Fuglevand & Archer (1994), *J. Electromyogr. Kinesiol.* 4:15–26 | Cross-talk via cross-correlation (instrumentation) |
| Hermens et al. (2000), *J. Electromyogr. Kinesiol.* 10:361–374 | SENIAM electrode placement standard |

## Relevance to migera

Only as provenance: the twitch-dynamics and eccentric-EMG papers are the
primary sources for the activation-filter and force-velocity design leads in
this chapter. The instrumentation papers are irrelevant to migera.
