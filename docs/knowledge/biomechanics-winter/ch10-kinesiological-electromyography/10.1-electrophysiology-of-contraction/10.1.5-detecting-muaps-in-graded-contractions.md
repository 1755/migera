# 10.1.5 Detection of motor unit action potentials from electromyogram during graded contractions

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §10.1.5, pp. 256–257 ·
> [open PDF at p. 256](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=269) ·
> Up: [10.1 Electrophysiology of muscle contraction](./INDEX.md)

Individual motor units can be pulled out of an EMG by decomposition
algorithms, but only a handful at a time and only those near the electrodes.

## Key ideas

- DeLuca et al. (1982): multiple indwelling electrodes identify individual
  m.u.a.p.'s in low-level graded contractions.
- Later: a quadrifilar needle (three channels) plus decomposition by template
  matching, template updating and firing statistics (DeLuca 1993).
- Erim et al. (1996) tracked up to four motor units' firing rates in tibialis
  anterior from 0 to 100% of **maximum voluntary contraction (MVC)**.
- Only units inside the array's pick-up region are seen. Surface EMG has also
  been decomposed (McGill et al. 1987).

## Where to read in the book

- pp. 256–257 (PDF 269–270): one paragraph, continuing past Fig. 10.4.
