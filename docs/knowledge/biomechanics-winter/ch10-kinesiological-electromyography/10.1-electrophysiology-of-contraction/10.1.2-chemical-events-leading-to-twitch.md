# 10.1.2 Sequence of chemical events leading to a twitch

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §10.1.2, p. 251 ·
> [open PDF at p. 251](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=264) ·
> Up: [10.1 Electrophysiology of muscle contraction](./INDEX.md)

The electrical event (m.u.a.p.) and the mechanical event (twitch) are linked by
a chemical cascade, which is why force always trails the EMG by tens of
milliseconds.

## Key ideas

- The m.u.a.p. starts at the Z line of the contractile element and spreads
  inward along the transverse tubular system.
- This releases Ca²⁺ from the sarcoplasmic reticulum; Ca²⁺ diffuses to the
  actin and myosin filaments.
- There ATP is hydrolysed to ADP + heat + mechanical energy (tension). The
  mechanical output is an **impulsive force at the cross-bridges**.
- The time course of that force is modelled in §9.0.5 (the twitch as a
  critically damped second-order impulse response).
