# 10.1.1 Motor end plate

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §10.1.1, p. 251 ·
> [open PDF at p. 251](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=264) ·
> Up: [10.1 Electrophysiology of muscle contraction](./INDEX.md)

Each motor unit is driven by one motoneuron through synaptic junctions called
motor end plates; normally every nerve action potential produces exactly one
motor unit action potential (m.u.a.p.).

## Key ideas

- The motoneuron is the "final common pathway". Its action potential releases
  a quantum of acetylcholine (ACh), which crosses a 200–500 Å synaptic gap and
  depolarizes the postsynaptic membrane.
- That depolarization, recordable with a microelectrode, is the **end plate
  potential (EPP)**. Normally it exceeds threshold and starts an action
  potential in the adjacent muscle fibre membrane.
- In neuromuscular transmission disorders (e.g. ACh depletion) the
  one-to-one relation breaks: the block may be complete, only at high
  stimulation rates, or intermittent.

## Equations and numbers

- Synaptic gap width: 200–500 Å.
