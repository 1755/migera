# 1.3.4 Anthropometry

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §1.3.4, p. 11 ·
> [open PDF at p. 11](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=24) ·
> Up: [1.3 Scope of the textbook](./INDEX.md)

Body and limb measurements were once thought outside biomechanics, but no
model can be built without them. An analysis is only as accurate as its
anthropometry, just as it is only as accurate as its kinematics and
kinetics.

## Key ideas

The data a model needs: segment masses, mass-centre locations, segment
lengths, joint centres of rotation, muscle angles of pull, muscle mass and
cross-sectional area, and moments of inertia.

## Where to read in the book

- p. 11 (PDF 24): the whole section.

## Relevance to migera

High for the ragdoll. The body masses, centre-of-mass offsets and inertias
of the 14 bodies in `src/character/anim/ragdoll.rs` are exactly these
parameters, and Chapter 4's tables give defensible fractions of total body
mass.
