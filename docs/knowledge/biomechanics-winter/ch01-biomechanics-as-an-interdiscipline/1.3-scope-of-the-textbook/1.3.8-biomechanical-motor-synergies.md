# 1.3.8 Biomechanical motor synergies

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §1.3.8, p. 12 ·
> [open PDF at p. 12](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=25) ·
> Up: [1.3 Scope of the textbook](./INDEX.md)

Better technology now allows analysis of complex whole-body movements.
Because adjacent muscle groups interact strongly, it has become necessary
to identify **synergies**: two or more groups acting together toward one
goal. The new Chapter 11 presents several techniques for this.

## Key ideas

- A synergy is the fourth integration level of §1.2 (Fig. 1.6): moments at
  several joints combined toward one goal.
- Chapter 11's examples are the support moment in walking, medial/lateral
  and anterior/posterior balance in standing, and dynamic balance in
  gait initiation and termination.

## Where to read in the book

- p. 12 (PDF 25): the whole section (one paragraph).

## Relevance to migera

Medium-high. Synergies are how to coordinate migera's per-joint systems
(leg IK, pelvis, foot lock, ragdoll PD) toward one balance goal instead of
tuning each separately. Chapter 11's inverted-pendulum balance results
feed pelvis sway and gait start and stop.
