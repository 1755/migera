# 9.4 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §9.4, pp. 247–249 ·
> [open PDF at p. 247](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=260) ·
> Up: [Chapter 9 — Muscle mechanics](./INDEX.md)

The chapter cites 36 works, mostly 1935–1996 physiology. The handful below are
the ones worth chasing for building or tuning muscle-like actuators.

## Most useful for animation and physics work

| Work | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Hill, A. V. (1938). "The Heat of Shortening and Dynamic Constants of Muscle." *Proc. R. Soc. B* 126:136–195. | The force-velocity hyperbola, Eq. 9.3; primary source for the curvature constant $a$ that Winter doesn't give. |
| Fenn, W. O. & Marsh, B. S. (1935). "Muscular Force at Different Speeds of Shortening." *J. Physiol.* 85:277–297. | The exponential force-velocity fit, Eq. 9.2; check the sign of its exponent here. |
| Gordon, A. M., Huxley, A. F. & Julian, F. J. (1966). *J. Physiol.* 184:170. | Sarcomere force-length curve (the 1.5/2.5/4.0 µm anchors). |
| Winters, J. M. (1990). "Hill-Based Muscle Models: A System Engineering Perspective." In *Multiple Muscle Systems*, Springer, pp. 69–93. | Engineering-oriented Hill models; source of the 1.1–1.8 Fmax eccentric plateau. |
| Zahalak, G. I. (1990). "Modelling Muscle Mechanics (and Energetics)." Same volume, pp. 1–23. | Cross-bridge-based alternative; isovelocity eccentric behaviour. |
| Milner-Brown, H. S., Stein, R. B. & Yemm, R. (1973a). *J. Physiol.* 228:285–306. | Twitch as a critically damped second-order impulse response (Eq. 9.1). |
| Buchthal, F. & Schmalbruch, H. (1970). *Acta Physiol. Scand.* 79:435–452. | Contraction times T per human muscle (table in §9.0.5). |
| Fuglevand, A. J., Winter, D. A. & Patla, A. E. (1993). "Models of Recruitment and Rate Coding Organization in Motor Unit Pools." *J. Neurophysiol.* 70:2470–2488. | A complete recruitment + rate-coding model, if force noise or fine grading is ever needed. |
| Fung, Y. C. (1971). *J. Biomech.* 4:289–295. | Equivalence of linear spring-damper layouts (Eq. 9.4). |
| van Ingen Schenau, G. J. (1984). *Human Movement Sci.* 3:301–336. | Why tendon elastic storage does not explain the prestretch benefit. |
| Winter, D. A. (1976). "Biomechanical Model Related EMG to Changing Isometric Tension." *Dig. 11th Int. Conf. Med. Biol. Eng.*, pp. 362–363. | The EMG-driven M–B–K model of §9.3.1. |

The rest cover motor-unit physiology and EMG (Henneman 1974a/b, DeLuca et al.
1982, Desmedt & Godaux 1978, Erim et al. 1996, Bellemare et al. 1983), passive
tissue models (Crowe 1970, Glantz 1974, Gottlieb & Agarwal 1971, Hatze 1978),
and single measurements cited in the text (Sale et al. 1982, Woittiez 1984,
Bahler 1967, Close 1965, Faulkner et al. 1980).

## Relevance to migera

Only as a pointer list: if migera adopts a Hill-type ceiling or activation
filter, Hill (1938) and Winters (1990) are where to get constants the book
leaves out.
