# 9.1.4 In vivo force-length measures

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §9.1.4, pp. 235–236 ·
> [open PDF at p. 235](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=248) ·
> Up: [9.1 Force-length characteristics of muscles](./INDEX.md)

In humans the force-length curve can only be seen indirectly, as **maximum
joint moment versus joint angle**, and that curve mixes several muscles and
changing moment arms. One study (Sale et al., 1982) isolated the soleus well
enough to read its curve: peak plantarflexor moment at **15° dorsiflexion**,
falling roughly **linearly to near zero at 30° plantarflexion**.

## Key ideas

- Two confounds make moment-angle curves ambiguous:
  1. An MVC of one agonist activates the other agonists too; each sits at a
     different point on its own force-length curve.
  2. Moment = force × moment arm, and **both** change with joint angle.
- Sale et al. (1982) design: ankle over 50° (30° plantarflexion to 20°
  dorsiflexion) with the **knee flexed 90°** to slacken the two-joint
  gastrocnemius, leaving the soleus dominant; the soleus moment arm was assumed
  roughly constant over that range.
- **Result:** MVC, peak twitch tension and 10 Hz tetanic stimulation all gave
  nearly the same curve — peak at 15° dorsiflexion, dropping roughly linearly to
  near zero at 30° plantarflexion. Interpreted as a soleus whose resting length
  is at 15° dorsiflexion and whose cross-bridges reach full overlap at 30°
  plantarflexion.

## Equations and numbers

| Ankle angle | Relative soleus moment (Sale et al., 1982) |
|---|---|
| 15° dorsiflexion | maximum |
| 30° plantarflexion | near zero |
| between | roughly linear |

## Relevance to migera

This is the kind of data an **angle-dependent strength curve** would be
authored from, and the lesson is that what you want for a ragdoll is exactly
the joint-level moment-angle curve (all agonists and moment arms lumped), not
per-muscle force-length. For the ankle it says plantarflexion strength is
highest with the foot dorsiflexed (early push-off) and vanishes toward full
plantarflexion. migera's foot ceiling in `default_joint_params`
(`src/character/anim/ragdoll.rs`) is constant; a ceiling multiplier ramping
from 1 at 15° dorsiflexion to ~0 at 30° plantarflexion, for plantarflexing
torque only, is directly derivable from this note. Not modeled today.
