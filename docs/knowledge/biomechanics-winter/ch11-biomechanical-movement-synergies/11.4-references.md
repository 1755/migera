# 11.4 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §11.4, p. 295 ·
> [open PDF at p. 295](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=308) ·
> Up: [Chapter 11 — Biomechanical movement synergies](./INDEX.md)

The chapter cites 15 works. Most of them are Winter-lab papers from Waterloo
on balance and gait. The list fits on one page (p. 295; PDF p. 309 is already
Appendix A).

## Most useful for animation and physics work

| Reference | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Winter, Prince, Frank, Powell & Zabjek (1996), "A unified theory regarding A/P and M/L balance during quiet stance", *J. Neurophysiol.* 75:2334–2343 | Source of the ankle (A/P) vs hip load/unload (M/L) split and Eq. 11.3; start here for a standing-balance controller |
| Winter, Patla, Prince, Ishac & Gielo-Perzak (1998), "Stiffness control of balance in quiet standing", *J. Neurophysiol.* 80:1211–1221 | The argument that quiet-standing balance is a stiffness (in-phase) law, not reactive feedback; gives the case for PD-style balance gains |
| Gage, Winter, Frank & Adkin (2003), "Kinematic and kinetic validity of the inverted pendulum model in quiet standing", *Gait & Posture* 19:124–132 | Quantifies how far the single inverted pendulum can be trusted |
| Jian, Winter, Ishac & Gilchrist (1993), "Trajectory of the body COG and COP during initiation and termination of gait", *Gait & Posture* 1:9–22 | Full COP/COM trajectories for starting and stopping (Figs. 11.8–11.9); primary data for start/stop transitions |
| Halliday, Winter, Frank, Patla & Prince (1998), "The initiation of gait in young, elderly and Parkinson's disease subjects", *Gait & Posture* 8:8–14 | Initiation patterns scale with final walking speed; supports one speed-parameterized start |
| MacKinnon & Winter (1993), "Control of whole body balance in the frontal plane during human walking", *J. Biomech.* 26:633–644 | Frontal-plane HAT balance by hip abductors in single support; the reference for lateral pelvis/trunk control while walking |
| Winter (1980), "Overall principle of lower limb support during stance phase of gait", *J. Biomech.* 13:923–927 | Original support moment paper |
| Winter (1984), "Kinematic and kinetic patterns in human gait: variability and compensating effects", *Human Movement Sci.* 3:51–76 | The hip–knee trade-off (compensation) data behind §11.1 |

## Other citations

- Eng, Winter, MacKinnon & Patla (1992), *Neurosci. Res. Communications* 11:73–80: anticipatory leg moments with arm movements.
- Rietdyk, Patla, Winter, Ishac & Little (1999), *J. Biomech.* 32:1149–1158: recovery from M/L upper-body perturbations.
- Horak & Nashner (1986), *J. Neurophysiol.* 55:1369–1381: ankle/hip strategies and the central programming of posture.
- Herman, Cook, Cozzens & Freedman (1973): postural reactions in gait initiation, in *Control of Posture and Locomotion*, Plenum.
- Nelson-Wong, Gregory, Winter & Callaghan (2008), *Clin. Biomech.* 23:545–553: gluteus medius co-activation predicts low back pain.
- Hunter & Kearney (1981), *Neurosci. Lett.* 25:155–159, and Winter, Lodge & Josenhans (1966), *Am. Heart J.* 71:666–670: respiratory and cardiac contributions to sway.
- Winter (1991), *The Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Gait*, 2nd ed., Waterloo Biomechanics: source of Figs. 11.1–11.3 and 11.7.

## Where to read in the book

## Relevance to migera

These are the primary sources to pull when building a balance controller for
the active ragdoll (Winter 1996/1998, Gage 2003) or start/stop transitions
(Jian 1993, Halliday 1998). Nothing here is needed to use the chapter notes.
