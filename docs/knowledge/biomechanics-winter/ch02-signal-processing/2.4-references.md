# 2.4 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §2.4, pp. 43–44 ·
> [open PDF at p. 43](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=56) ·
> Up: [Chapter 2 — Signal processing](./INDEX.md)

Eleven references; five are directly useful to procedural locomotion and
balance work, the rest are EMG or general signal-processing texts.

## Key ideas

Most useful for migera:

| Reference | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Winter, D. A., H. G. Sidwall, D. A. Hobson (1974). "Measurement and Reduction of Noise in Kinematics of Locomotion." *J. Biomech.* 7: 157–159. | Source of the gait harmonic spectrum (99.7% of toe power in 7 harmonics) and the 6 Hz cutoff — [2.2.4](./2.2-frequency-analysis/2.2.4-spectrum-analysis-applications.md). |
| Winter, D. A. (1995). *The Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Gait: Normal, Elderly and Pathological*, 2nd ed. Waterloo Biomechanics. | Normative ensemble-averaged gait curves (angles, moments, powers) — the reference bands of [2.3.1](./2.3-ensemble-averaging/2.3.1-ensemble-averaged-profiles.md). |
| Prince, F., D. A. Winter, P. Stergiou, S. E. Walt (1994). "Anticipatory Control of Upper Body Balance during Human Locomotion." *Gait and Posture* 2: 19–25. | Top-down paraspinal timing and head vs. hip acceleration — [2.1.8](./2.1-auto-and-cross-correlation/2.1.8-cross-correlation-applications.md). |
| Winter, D. A. (1982). "Camera Speeds for Normal and Pathological Gait Analysis." *Med. Biol. Eng. Comput.* 20: 408–412. | Evidence that 24–25 Hz sampling suffices for walking. |
| Gage, W. G., D. A. Winter, J. S. Frank (2004). "Kinematic and Kinetic Validation of Inverted Pendulum Model in Quiet Standing." *Gait and Posture* 19: 124–132. | Inverted-pendulum COM/COP model of standing sway. |

Others cited:
- Bobet, J. & R. W. Norman (1990), EMG-to-joint-moment identification, *J. Biomech.* 23: 1275–1276 — coherence/transfer functions.
- Brigham, E. O. (1974), *The Fast Fourier Transform*, Prentice-Hall — FFT text.
- Carpenter, M. G. et al. (2001), sampling duration effects on COP measures, *Gait and Posture* 13: 35–40 — ≥ 1 min standing records.
- Nelson-Wong, E. et al. (2008), gluteus medius activation and low back pain in standing, *Clinical Biomechanics* 23: 545–553.
- Rader, C. M. & B. Gold (1967), digital filter design in the frequency domain, *Proc. IEEE* 55: 149–171 (printed "Radar").
- Wieman, C. (1991), MSc thesis, Univ. of Waterloo — elderly vs. young trunk EMG in gait.

## Where to read in the book

- pp. 43–44 (PDF 56–57): the full list.

## Relevance to migera

Winter 1995 is the one worth acquiring if migera ever builds a
gait-plausibility test with human reference bands for joint angles; Prince
et al. 1994 is the source for head-stabilization behaviour.
