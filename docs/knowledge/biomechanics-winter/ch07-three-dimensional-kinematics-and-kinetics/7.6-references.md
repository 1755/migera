# 7.6 References

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §7.6, pp. 198–199 ·
> [open PDF at p. 198](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=211) ·
> Up: [Chapter 7 — Three-dimensional kinematics and kinetics](./INDEX.md)

Three works, all on gait analysis.

## Key ideas

| Work | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Davis, R. B., S. Õunpuu, D. Tyburski and J. R. Gage, "A gait analysis data collection and reduction technique," *Human Movement Sci.* 10:575–587, 1991 | The standard clinical marker set and joint-centre offset protocol ("Helen Hayes/Davis" model) referred to in §7.2; source for how clinical 3D joint angles are defined. |
| Eng, J. J. and D. A. Winter, "Kinetic analysis of the lower limbs during walking: what information can be gained from a three-dimensional model," *J. Biomech.* 28:753–758, 1995 | Source of Figs 7.4–7.5; the primary data for 3D ankle/knee/hip moments and powers in walking — most useful for gait/ragdoll targets. |
| Õunpuu, S., R. B. Davis and P. A. DeLuca, "Joint kinetics: methods, interpretation and treatment decision-making in children with cerebral palsy and myelomeningocele," *Gait & Posture* 4:62–78, 1996 | Clinical interpretation of joint kinetics; low relevance for animation. |
