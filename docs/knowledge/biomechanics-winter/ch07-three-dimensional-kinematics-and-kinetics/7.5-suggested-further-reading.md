# 7.5 Suggested further reading

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §7.5, p. 198 ·
> [open PDF at p. 198](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=211) ·
> Up: [Chapter 7 — Three-dimensional kinematics and kinetics](./INDEX.md)

Three texts for going beyond the chapter's single-segment 3D dynamics.

## Key ideas

| Work | What it is for |
|---|---|
| D'Sousa, A. F. and V. J. Garg, *Advanced Dynamics Modeling and Analysis* (Prentice-Hall, 1984) | General multibody dynamics modelling. |
| Greenwood, D. T., *Principles of Dynamics*, 2nd ed. (Prentice-Hall, 1988), ch. 7 | Rigid-body 3D dynamics: Euler angles, Euler's equations, gyroscopic motion — the full derivation behind [7.4.2](./7.4-kinetic-analysis-reactions-and-moments/7.4.2-euler-3d-equations-of-motion.md). |
| Zatsiorsky, V. M., *Kinetics of Human Motion* (Human Kinetics, 2002) | Human-specific 3D kinetics; the most directly useful for character work (joint moments, inertia, 3D joint coordinate systems). |

## Where to read in the book

- p. 198 (PDF 211): the list.

## Relevance to migera

Pointers only. Greenwood ch. 7 is the reference to reach for if ragdoll
tumbling/precession needs to be understood beyond Eq. 7.9; Zatsiorsky for
joint-coordinate-system conventions when importing gait-lab angles.
