# 7.4.4 Joint mechanical powers

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §7.4.4, pp. 194–195 ·
> [open PDF at p. 194](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=207) ·
> Up: [7.4 Kinetic analysis of reaction forces and moments](./INDEX.md)

The 2D joint power M·ω becomes a sum over the three anatomical components:
power at the distal and proximal joints is moment · joint angular velocity.
Positive means the muscle group is generating mechanical energy, negative
that it is absorbing it.

## Equations and numbers

$$P_d = M_{xd}\,\omega_{xd} + M_{yd}\,\omega_{yd} + M_{zd}\,\omega_{zd}\qquad(7.10a)$$
$$P_p = M_{xp}\,\omega_{xp} + M_{yp}\,\omega_{yp} + M_{zp}\,\omega_{zp}\qquad(7.10b)$$

Moments in N·m, angular velocities in rad/s, power in W.

- ω_{·d}, ω_{·p} are **joint** angular velocities (not shown in Fig. 7.3 and
  not defined further in the text). The standard definition — and the one
  consistent with the 2D chapters — is the relative angular velocity of the
  two segments meeting at the joint, both expressed in the same frame as the
  moment. The dot product is frame-independent only if both vectors are in
  the same frame.
- The book cites "Equation (5.5)" for the 2D form; Eq. 5.5 in this edition is
  a centre-of-pressure formula, so the cross-reference is wrong. The 2D joint
  power P = M·ω_j is developed in [§6.4](../../ch06-work-energy-and-power/6.4-power-balances/INDEX.md).

## Where to read in the book

- pp. 194–195 (PDF 207–208): Eqs 7.10a–b and the sign interpretation.

## Relevance to migera

- For a PD-driven ragdoll joint, P = τ·(ω_child − ω_parent) tells whether
  the controller is driving the motion (P > 0) or braking it (P < 0). A PD
  joint that shows sustained positive power while the character is at rest is
  pumping energy in — a direct, cheap instability diagnostic alongside the
  error/spin checks in
  [full-strength read-back hides the physics](../../../character-animation/ragdoll-and-physics/full-strength-readback-hides-the-physics.md).
- Procedural gait design: the big generation bursts (ankle push-off A2-S, hip
  pull-off H3-S; [7.4.5](./7.4.5-sample-moment-and-power-curves.md)) mark where
  a physically driven walk must inject energy.
