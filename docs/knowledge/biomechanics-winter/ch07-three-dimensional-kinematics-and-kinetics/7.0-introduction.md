# 7.0 Introduction

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §7.0, p. 176 ·
> [open PDF at p. 176](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=189) ·
> Up: [Chapter 7 — Three-dimensional kinematics and kinetics](./INDEX.md)

Commercial 3D capture (multi-camera TV systems with passive reflective markers,
or active infrared-diode markers) produces one thing: x, y, z of every marker
at every sample, in the lab's global reference system. The chapter turns those
into segment anatomical frames and then runs the same kinetic analysis as the
2D chapters.

## Key ideas

- Input: marker trajectories in the GRS; output: per-segment orientation
  angles, COM trajectories, joint forces, moments and powers.
- The steps mirror 2D: kinematics (Chapter 3) → link-segment kinetics
  (Chapter 5) → power (Chapter 6), with rotation matrices added between
  global and segment frames.

## Where to read in the book

- p. 176 (PDF 189): the one-paragraph introduction.

## Relevance to migera

Low by itself; migera has no marker data. It establishes that everything in
Chapter 7 is about *frames*, which is the axis along which migera's rig bugs
have occurred (see the chapter INDEX).
