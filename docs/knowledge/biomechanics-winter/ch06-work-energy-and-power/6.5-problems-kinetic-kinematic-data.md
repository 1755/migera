# 6.5 Problems based on kinetic and kinematic data

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §6.5, pp. 173–174 ·
> [open PDF at p. 173](../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=186) ·
> Up: [Chapter 6 — Mechanical work, energy, and power](./INDEX.md)

Every problem uses the single-subject walking trial of Appendix A (stride
frames 28–97, 68–69 frames, right heel contact at 28, toe-off at 70) and has
its answer printed in Table A.6 (segment energies) or Table A.7 (powers and
transfers). That makes them ready-made test fixtures: known inputs, known
outputs, independent of any migera code.

## What each problem exercises

| # | Task | Uses | Check against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PE, translational KE, rotational KE of leg (frame 20) and thigh (frame 70) | Eqs 6.14–6.17; segment kinematics (A.2–A.3) and anthropometrics (Ch. 4) | Table A.6 |
| 2 | Plot leg, thigh, HAT energy components over the stride; is each conservative? HAT % conservation by Eq. 6.18 vs exact Eqs 6.19–6.22 | segment energy time series | Table A.6 |
| 3 | Total body energy at frame 28, using the right side half a stride (34 frames) later for the left; do lower limbs or HAT dominate metabolic demand? | Eq. 6.23, symmetry assumption | Table A.6 |
| 4a | Muscle power at ankle (frames 30, 50, 65), knee (35, 40, 65, 20), hip (50, 70, 4); name the muscle group | Eq. 6.27 with segment ω (A.7) and moments (A.5) | Table A.7 |
| 4b | Where is the major generation in walking, when, by which muscles? Do knee extensors generate much? Which hip group powers swing, when? | scanning A.7 | Table A.7 (answers summarized in [6.3.1](./6.3-internal-and-external-work-calculation/6.3.1-internal-work-calculation.md)) |
| 5 | Passive transfer across ankle (20, 33, 65), knee (2, 20, 65), hip (2, 20, 67); which direction? | Eq. 6.6, Fig. 6.8 (book misprints "Eq. 6.10") | Table A.7 |
| 6 | Power balance for foot (20), leg (20, 65), thigh (63) | Eq. 6.28, Fig. 6.19b | Table A.6 energy rate |
| 7 | Muscle transfer leg/foot (60), thigh/leg (7, 35) with direction | Table 6.2 | Table A.7 |

Short answers already known from Table A.7: problem 4b(i) — ankle
plantarflexors at push-off, peak 272.4 W at frame 64 just before toe-off;
4b(ii) — the knee extensors generate only a small burst (~21 W peak,
frames 38–44); 4b(iii) — hip flexors pull the thigh into swing around
toe-off and early swing (≈51 W at frame 2).

## Where to read in the book

## Relevance to migera

Direct test fixtures for any energetics code in migera (a segment-energy
function, a joint-power probe, a per-body power balance): feed the appendix
kinematics and forces in, assert Table A.6/A.7 values out. This is an
independent reference in the sense of the
[vacuous-test lesson](../../engineering-practice/testing/same-function-both-sides-is-a-vacuous-test.md)
— the expected numbers do not come from the code under test. Remember the
Example 6.5 typo ([6.4.2](./6.4-power-balances/6.4.2-power-balance-within-segments.md))
before trusting the book's worked arithmetic over its tables.
