# 6.0.2 Law of conservation of energy

> **Source:** Winter (2009) §6.0.2, pp. 140–141 ·
> [open PDF at p. 140](../../../../books/bmcoh/BIOMECHANICS_AND_MOTOR_CONTROL_OF_HUMAN.pdf#page=153) ·
> Up: [6.0 Introduction](./INDEX.md)

A segment changes energy only through flows across its boundaries — tendons,
ligaments, joint contact surfaces — so the sum of all those flows over an
interval must equal the segment's energy change. The same statement in rate
form is a *power balance*. Independently, energy can shift between the
segment's potential and kinetic stores without any boundary flow.

## Key ideas

- **Six routes in Fig. 6.1:** a segment with proximal and distal joint
  contacts and four muscle attachments has six places energy can enter or
  leave.
- **Energy form (over Δt):** flows +4, +2.4, +5.3, −1.7, −0.2, −3.8 J give
  $\Delta E_s = 6.0\ \text{J}$.
- **Power form (instantaneous):** with Δt = 20 ms the same flows are +200,
  +120, +265, −85, −10, −190 W, so $\Delta E_s/\Delta t = 300\ \text{W}$.
- **It is a cross-check.** Computing each flow (joint force × joint velocity,
  moment × angular velocity) and separately computing the segment's energy
  from kinematics gives two independent answers. They never match exactly:
  measurement error plus the link-segment model's idealizations (rigid
  segments, frictionless pin joints) leave a residual.
- **Within-segment exchange** is a second, separate conservation mechanism:
  the total $E_s$ can stay fixed while PE, translational KE and rotational KE
  trade among themselves (quantified in
  [6.2.1](../6.2-forms-energy-storage/6.2.1-segment-energy-and-within-segment-exchange.md)).

## Equations and numbers

$$\Delta E_s = \sum_{k} W_k \qquad \frac{dE_s}{dt} = \sum_k P_k$$

where $W_k$ (J) and $P_k$ (W) are the work and power at each attachment,
positive into the segment. The full four-term version is Eq. 6.28 in
[6.4.2](../6.4-power-balances/6.4.2-power-balance-within-segments.md).

## Relevance to migera

This is the basis for an **energy-balance oracle** on the avian ragdoll: per
body, $dE_s/dt$ from its state should equal the sum of joint-constraint power
plus PD-torque power. A persistent positive residual means the integrator or
the controller is injecting energy — the same failure the
[PD damping bound](../../../character-animation/ragdoll-and-physics/pd-damping-explicit-integration-bound.md)
describes, and the "no energy growth" guards in `src/character/anim/ragdoll_plugin.rs`
test coarsely via angular velocity. Expect a nonzero residual from solver
compliance, so assert a tolerance, not equality.
