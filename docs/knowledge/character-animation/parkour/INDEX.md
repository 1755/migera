# Parkour

The moves a Prince-of-Persia-style platformer needs, built as procedural
animation against the level's geometry. The design comes first; notes on
each move as it is built follow it.

| Note | What it establishes | Read when |
|---|---|---|
| [Parkour moves, step by step](./parkour-moves-implementation-design.md) | The step-by-step design: foundations shared with the jump and ladder, then ten steps from grabbing a ledge to crawling, each with its model, data, API, tests and done criteria | before starting or changing any parkour move |

| [A ledge is caught near the top of a jump and hung from](./a-ledge-is-caught-near-the-top-of-a-jump-and-hung-from.md) | Step 1: a standing jump up and in, the hands meeting the lip before its top; the arms' give and a pendulum about the grip; braced on the wall or free; hands hooked over the lip. Traps: grips in their own frame, the lip at the knuckle, the walk's stop, the approach's side entry | before changing `parkour/hang.rs`, `hand::hooked`, or any hold on an edge |
| [A hang is climbed up from by a pull, a press and a step on](./a-hang-is-climbed-up-from-by-pull-press-and-step-on.md) | Step 2: the hips on a C² spline through shapes measured from the hands (pull, turn over, press folded over the top, step on, stand); feet up the face then over; a mid-swing ask waits. Traps: a knee's arc round its socket into the corner, a thigh turned round at the hip, the hips joint vs the root translation | before changing `parkour/hang/up.rs`, or any move bringing a leg up over an edge |
| [A ledge is shimmied hand over hand, and round corners](./a-ledge-is-shimmied-hand-over-hand-and-round-corners.md) | Step 3: hand over hand, pulled up, the hips on the hang's sideways spring; at a corner the hands match up and the body rides the one rigid motion between the two hangs. Traps: the facing turn composed twice (hidden by half turns), feet measured off the face only, per-kind corner timing | before changing `parkour/hang/shimmy.rs`, or turning a body at a corner |
| [A drop is fallen ballistically and landed to the measured time and depth](./a-drop-is-fallen-ballistically-and-landed-to-the-measured-time-and-depth.md) | Step 4, first part: walking off an edge falls under gravity and lands as measured (time and knee depth keyed by drop height, braking `v(1-s)^n(1+n·s)`). Traps: the study's touchdown speeds are not free fall's, a gap measured a frame early, the ground snap hiding drops | before changing `parkour/fall.rs`, any landing, or the walker's ground snap |
| [A hang is dropped into, let go of, and caught from a fall](./a-hang-is-dropped-into-let-go-of-and-caught-from-a-fall.md) | Step 5: dropping down runs the climb-up backward, slower; letting go falls, onto a step under the feet if there is room; a jump over an edge falls on at its centre of mass's velocity; any fall catches a ledge in reach, swept over the frame. Traps: a frame-end-only catch, the hips not ballistic in a jump, the root over the edge before take-off, overlapping blocks | before changing the hand-offs between `parkour/hang.rs`, `hang/up.rs`, `fall.rs` and the walker's jump |
| [A hang is leapt from, up, aside or back, as a launch and a ballistic flight](./a-hang-is-leapt-from-up-aside-or-back.md) | Step 6: a launch in the hang (the hips on a Hermite curve to the release velocity, the hands letting go over the last 0.12 s), then a `Falling` aimed so the shoulders come just under the target's lip, at most 3 m/s across; back turns round in the air. Traps: hands held to the lip till release, each part's own swing over the hand-off, a release inside the wall hold's give, the landing's room before the catch | before changing `parkour/hang/leap.rs`, the release hand-off, or the catch sweep |
| [A block is mantled as a climb up from the floor](./a-block-is-mantled-as-a-climb-up-from-the-floor.md) | Step 7, first part: a mantle onto a block 0.85 m to chest high is the climb-up's plan with a new head (hands on as the knees dip, a drive off the floor, feet onto the face) then its press, step on and stand. Traps: feet planted past the drive, a foot path bowing into the face, the lean at a shoulder-high wall | before changing `Hanging::mantle` or the climb-up's plan |
| [A low obstacle is speed-vaulted as a reshaped running leap](./a-low-obstacle-is-speed-vaulted-as-a-reshaped-running-leap.md) | Step 7, second part: a running leap whose flight is reshaped (hips rolled, legs tucked out to the side, trunk leant onto a hand on the top), moved whole to keep the leap's COM; the plan checks its own clearance; the walker adjusts its last steps to the take-off. Traps: frames, pops, a hand target that creeps, legs not carried with the roll | before changing `parkour/vault.rs` or the walker's vault |
| [A wall is run up off one foot and its lip caught](./a-wall-is-run-up-off-one-foot-and-its-lip-caught.md) | Step 8, first part: a braked running leap, one foot on the face 1 m up for 0.37 s, the hips on a Hermite curve, then a fall aimed at the lip; catches up to 2.6 m. Traps: Lawson's rise out of the leg's reach, arms swinging into the wall, a knee aimed two ways, the fall's arms dropping in a frame | before changing `parkour/wall.rs` |
| [A wall is kicked off toward a lip round a corner](./a-wall-is-kicked-off-toward-a-lip-round-a-corner.md) | Step 8, second part: the tic-tac, the run up's take-off and foot on the wall (1.2 m up, off the far foot), the hips leaving where a steady push brings them, then a fall turned to another wall's lip; catches 2.3-2.7 m from 0.6-0.9 rad off square, taken off 0.2 m near to 0.1 m far. Traps: a squeezed arm swing, knee keep-off passes, a straight leg's IK hinge, an elbow into the wall, a test at only the best take-off | before changing `WallRun::kick` or the walker's kick |
| [A wall is run along on two steps of a lifted leap](./a-wall-is-run-along-on-two-steps-of-a-lifted-leap.md) | Step 8, third part: a running leap that lands running, held up by two sin² pushes off the face (`Lift`, 1.3 m/s), reshaped onto it: leant off, each foot on a hold sole to the face as the body passes. Traps: a hold chased running back past the body, knees aimed up into the face, a foot's norm compounding through passes | before changing `parkour/along.rs`, `jump::Lift` or the walker's run along |
| [A wall is slid down as a braked fall](./a-wall-is-slid-down-as-a-braked-fall.md) | Step 8, fourth part: from a braced hang, the let-go fall with half its gravity braked off (`Falling::slide`), palms on the face above the shoulders, feet braced for 60 % of the flight; landed as from half the drop (6.5 m lands, letting go is fatal). Traps: hands pulled down too fast, the face let go in a frame, an arm's pole through straight | before changing `Falling::slide`, `Hanging::slide_down` or a fall's hands on a wall |
| [A bar is swung on, pumped, and let go of at a bar ahead](./a-bar-is-swung-on-pumped-and-let-go-of-at-a-bar-ahead.md) | Step 9, first part: a bar is a ledge with nothing below; the free hang's pendulum pumped on its energy to 1.2 rad, the trunk turned whole, hands rolling round the bar, legs piking and arching; a lache waits in its window for the least change that catches the bar ahead (1.5-2 m level, 2.4 m lower). Traps: a shared lean bending the trunk, hands hooked fixed, the leap's catch point, bars read from above as walls | before changing `parkour/hang/swing.rs`, a free hang's pose, or the walker's wall probes |
| [A pole is climbed as an inchworm, hands over a leg clamp](./a-pole-is-climbed-as-an-inchworm-hands-over-a-leg-clamp.md) | Step 9, second part: jumped onto, hands one above the other and the feet clamping; climbed in 0.6 m cycles of 1.8 s (pull as the legs fold, stand as the hands go up), down by the cycle reversed, slid down braked, gone round, let go of. Traps: facing along the bearing out (away), a hand's path through its shoulder, a stroke past the hands' reach | before changing `parkour/pole.rs` or the walker's pole |
| [A beam is walked with the feet near its line and the arms out](./a-beam-is-walked-with-the-feet-near-its-line-and-the-arms-out.md) | Step 10, first part: on a beam, the feet 0.4 of their step width apart, 0.7 m/s, arms out 75° swaying against the trunk, steered onto its line; taken from a step below beside it. Traps: an elbow bent up, the arm swing kept, a smoothly changing width rebuilding cycles | before changing `parkour/beam.rs` or the walk's step width |
| [A teeter at an edge windmills the arms, once a stop](./a-teeter-at-an-edge-windmills-the-arms-once-a-stop.md) | Step 10, second part: stopped with a drop of over 0.5 m 0.3 m ahead, a 1.6 s layer circles both arms twice and rocks the trunk, the feet planted, once a stop | before changing `parkour/teeter.rs` |
| [A fall facing a wall is held off it](./a-fall-facing-a-wall-is-held-off-it.md) | A fall has no collision: facing a wall that reaches the ground, its hips stop 0.25 m off (a 0.2 m give, about 2.3 g), its landing's room is planned over time, its hands and toes are kept off, the hurt lean dropped; a hang caught there keeps hips and knees out | before changing a fall's path, a landing near a wall, or a caught hang's legs |
| [Walking is kept out of walls](./walking-is-kept-out-of-walls.md) | The walker had no collision: ground read from above higher than a step pushes the body (0.2 m) back out, sliding along; a wall ahead is gone round by the least clear turn and back to its line, or stopped at in a dead end; tops found through a 1 m grid, cost flat in the level's size | before changing how the walker moves its root, or the ledge ground |
